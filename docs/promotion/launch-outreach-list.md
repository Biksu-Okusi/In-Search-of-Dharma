## Cautions

- "Dharmic" is also Hindu-nationalist vocabulary (RSS usage noted in the name check of 27 Sep).
  Do not approach figures from that camp; the book's non-privileging stance is the opposite.
- Critics (Payne, Purser, Sujato, Thanissaro, Bodhi) are worth a copy. A serious hostile review from a named combatant is better publicity than silence, and Part 6 already meets them.
- The anti-authoritarian lean of the sources is by design. Do not pitch the book as "balanced".
- The AI methodology statement sent to Ramsey on 24 Sep is canonical. Every pitch that mentions AI follows it; never contradict it.
