## Overall Assessment

The author addresses a complex and worthwhile subject with evident care and intellectual depth. The manuscript is compelling and instructive. In essence, this is a scholarly-anthropological, yet personal exploration of the word dharma, one that seeks a universal meaning distinct from the term's specifically Buddhist definition.

## Editorial Thoughts

1. **Preface Structure** – The author commendably opens with a reflection on the socio-political and personal biases he brings to the work—an important acknowledgment for the reader. However, the central thesis—the elaboration of the concept of dharma and what the book reveals—is introduced later in the preface. To strengthen the reader's initial engagement, I suggest restructuring the preface: begin much earlier with a clear, compelling statement of the book's core inquiry or contribution. This placement will frame the author's intellectual journey and immediately orient the reader to the manuscript's overarching goal.

2. **Audience & Positioning** – This is a thoughtful, well-researched work that will resonate with Tuwhiri's secular dharma readership. It tackles the ambitious task of exploring dharma as a universal concept, a topic many in this circle will find personally resonant. His thesis should provide the reader with thought-provoking ideas about why this universal exploration matters in today's world—connecting its insights to secular dharma's focus on ethics, meaning, and social cohesion.
