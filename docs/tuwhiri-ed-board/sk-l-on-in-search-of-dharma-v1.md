# Starr Kelton-Locke on 'In search of dharma' (v1)

- Source: `SK-L on ‘In Search of Dharma’ v1.pdf`
- Reviewer: Starr Kelton-Locke (SK-L), Tuwhiri editorial board
- Version: v1 (superseded by v2, see `sk-l-in-search-of-dharma-v2.md`)

## Overall assessment

The author tackles a complex and worthy topic with evident care and intellectual depth. I found much of the writing compelling and learned a great deal. In short, this is a personal, yet academic-anthropological take on the word dharma—one that strives for a universal meaning beyond the term's specifically Buddhist definition.

However, I have reservations about the book's marketability to a general audience in today's landscape. While I believe the project merits consideration by the board, I think it will need some editorial work to broaden its appeal.

## Key editorial suggestions

1. **Audience and flow** – The manuscript currently pulls from multiple philosophical traditions (e.g., Wittgenstein) and contains some dense, academically inclined passages. To reach readers beyond specialists, the argument should be streamlined to better reach the target "general intelligent reader," especially in the author's biographical preface and chapter 3.

2. **Preface and "burying the lede"** – In the preface, the author opens with a lengthy description of the socio-political-personal biases he brings to the work. In journalistic terms, this buries the lede – the most compelling part of the story (why dharma matters, what the book will reveal) is delayed. I suggest the preface start instead with a clear, engaging statement of the book's central question or contribution, then contextualize the author's position later.

3. **Clarity of purpose** – The author skillfully employs philosophical and anthropological ideas to expand the concept of dharma beyond conventional boundaries—an approach I found compelling, albeit my concern is that many might find this somewhat dense. I think to enhance accessibility, some revisions might be necessary while preserving the work's intellectual rigor.

4. **Bottom line** – This is a thoughtful, well-researched manuscript that fits a more niche audience as-is. With focused revisions to improve narrative flow, accessibility, and upfront engagement, it could reach a broader readership.
