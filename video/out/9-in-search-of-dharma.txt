In Search of Dharma — 9: Coda

Eight parts of searching have earned one plain definition and two tests. So here they are, stated rather than argued: what a dharma is, and how to tell the better ones ...

In Search of Dharma asks what a dharma is, how dharmas arise, how they get under the skin, how they go wrong, and what survives in a secular age. Preface, eight Parts and a Coda, read in full.
