In Search of Dharma — 8: Creating Dharmas

We have always created our dharmas and told ourselves we found them. What becomes possible, and what becomes dangerous, once we admit it and start building on purpose?

In Search of Dharma asks what a dharma is, how dharmas arise, how they get under the skin, how they go wrong, and what survives in a secular age. Preface, eight Parts and a Coda, read in full.
