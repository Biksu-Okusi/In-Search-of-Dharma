In Search of Dharma — 7: Dharmas in a Secular Age

Hundreds of millions of people now say they are "spiritual but not religious." That is not the absence of a dharma. It is the search for one, carried out by people who have lost the old foundations but kept the old need.

In Search of Dharma asks what a dharma is, how dharmas arise, how they get under the skin, how they go wrong, and what survives in a secular age. Preface, eight Parts and a Coda, read in full.
