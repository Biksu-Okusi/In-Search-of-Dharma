In Search of Dharma — 1: Defining Dharma

Dharma: A word almost no one can define without a lot of hand-waving, and almost everyone uses anyway. At its root, it means something very plain, "to hold"; the interesting thing is how far this plain idea reaches.

In Search of Dharma asks what a dharma is, how dharmas arise, how they get under the skin, how they go wrong, and what survives in a secular age. Preface, eight Parts and a Coda, read in full.
