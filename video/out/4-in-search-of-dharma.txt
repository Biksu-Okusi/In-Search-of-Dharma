In Search of Dharma — 4: A World of Dharmas

A relational web, a rational fortress, a sung land, an open hand, a commanding covenant: five ways of living that disagree about almost everything, and rhyme underneath. Including, if you have the nerve to look, your own.

In Search of Dharma asks what a dharma is, how dharmas arise, how they get under the skin, how they go wrong, and what survives in a secular age. Preface, eight Parts and a Coda, read in full.
