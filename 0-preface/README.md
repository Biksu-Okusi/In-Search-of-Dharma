# Preface (Part 0) — Author & Method Research

This directory holds the Stage-1 research notes for the **Preface (Part 0)** of the
*In Search of Dharma* essay series: the author's life, formation, intellectual
lineage, community, thesis, and the knowledgebase method behind the whole project.

These notes are kept **out of** the 52-question master registry
([`../Questions.md`](../Questions.md)) **by design**. The registry tracks the
project's research *questions about dharma*; Part 0 is research about the *author and
the method* (Gary Dean, a.k.a. Biksu Okusi, and the `appliedanthropology` /
`seculardharma` corpus the series draws on). They use the same note template,
frontmatter keys, five-section shape and `[S#]` citation discipline as every other DD
note (see [`../1-foundational/_template.md`](../1-foundational/_template.md) and
[`../METHODOLOGY.md`](../METHODOLOGY.md)), but they carry their own `0.x` ID space and
the `Preface` category, and so are excluded from the `Questions.md` dashboard.

Much of Part 0 rests on **primary self-report** — the author's own public site, his
own contributions to the corpus, and a private email thread (cited for substance
only, with all personal contact details stripped and never reproduced). Each note
flags that provenance honestly, names the few genuinely third-party corroborations,
and marks the project's secular / evolutionary / Western-academic corpus bias where
it shaped the answer.

## Publication status

Five notes are published here as **redacted public editions** (0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5,
0.6). A public edition excludes personal contact details, private third-party
specifics, and passages resting on the author's private records; every exclusion is
disclosed in the note where it is made, and the standing limits hold across all of
Part 0: the author's children are referred to by relationship only, and no personal
identifiers are reproduced anywhere.

Two notes are **held back for now**: 0.4 (Network & Publishing) rests on private
correspondence with living colleagues and awaits their review; 0.7 (Influences &
Acknowledgments) is in revision to remove a roster of private individuals
reconstructed from the mail archive. Public editions of both will follow.

Permanently private, and not part of this repository: the seed correspondence
(`_seed.md`), the mail archive (`.mail/`), and the unredacted originals of these
notes. Where a source entry is marked **private**, the chain of evidence ends —
deliberately and visibly — at material held by the author: the same
"personal communication" terminus scholarly citation has always allowed, disclosed
rather than implied.
